Hand-Controlled Robot Arm

Your hand is the controller. Webcam → hand tracking → inverse kinematics → physics, all in your browser. Video never leaves your machine.

Drive a 6-DOF robot arm with your hand

  • Move your hand — the arm follows it in 3D (toward/away = reach)
  • Pinch thumb + index — close the gripper, pick up a cube
  • Fist — clutch: freeze the arm to reposition your hand
  • Tilt your palm — the wrist follows

Companion to the Python + MuJoCo implementation — source on GitHub.
