Hand-Controlled Robot Arm

Video never leaves your machine.

Autopilot is driving — no camera in use.

A 6-DOF arm driven by your hand — inverse kinematics and physics, running live in the browser.

  • Hold still half a second to calibrate
  • Move your hand — the arm follows in 3D
  • Pinch to grab, tilt your palm to rotate the wrist
  • Fist to freeze the arm, open to resume

Companion to the Python + MuJoCo implementation — source on GitHub.
